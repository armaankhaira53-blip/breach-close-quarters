Supply and equipment rules

All magazines and reserves reset at wave start. RPG: 1 loaded + 8 spare rockets, 1.5-second reload, 2-second firing cooldown. Fire down at the ground near your feet to rocket jump: about 4m lift, 10 health cost, and 65% faster airborne movement until landing. Requires headroom; no midair boost stacking. Practice jumps cost no health. Sprint has no stamina limit. Slides last 0.7 seconds, slow down, and travel in a locked direction. Press C again to cancel into running, or Space to cancel into a jump when clear. Holding C never extends a slide. Machete: 25 damage, 1.65m reach, 1-second attack cooldown; no slide execution. Karambit: 60 damage and improved sliding contact; sliding hits are lethal to non-SWAT enemies. All SWAT variants are immune to karambit damage. Equipment resets to two uses; boogie bombs receive one, throwing knives four, C4 three. C4: G or left click in slot 3 places on a surface within 3m; V detonates any placed charges (1, 2 or 3). Sticky bombs use a 2.5-second fuse. Molotov: 30 impact damage plus 4 damage/second for 10 seconds. LMG: crouch and hold fire for 1 second to spin up; movement is locked until release. Pump shotgun: 5 shells, 3.4-second full reload. Fire axe: 1.1-second windup, 1.9-second attack cycle. Nerf foam darts deal zero damage. Choose your loadout from the main menu. Switching slots never grants supplies.

Flashbang: 5-second knockdown. Boogie: 5-second horse-riding dance. Smoke: 10-second cloud; NPCs entering it are blinded for 5 seconds. Smoke from either side blocks everyone's sight. Bandages heal 35 after 2.4 seconds; changing slots cancels the use without refund.
