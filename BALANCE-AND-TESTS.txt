RESUPPLY
At wave start, magazines reset to capacity; reserves reset, never accumulate.
Reserve factor = max(1, count/8 x difficulty stage), rounded UP to a full magazine.
Stage = 1 before wave 10, 1.12 at 10–14, 1.25 from 15 onward.
At wave 50: AR-4 30+360; pistol 15+120; sniper 5+60; burst 24+288;
double barrel 2+86; SMG 32+512; revolver 6+96. These are per-wave allocations.
RPG always 1 loaded + 8 reserve on every wave: 9 total rockets, independent of wave size.
Equipment: two uses per wave, except boogie bomb: one. Swapping slots never refills.
Clearing a wave awards credits at the 0.8-second threshold; the next wave starts
after four seconds and performs one resupply. Health restores by 35 at wave start.
Emergency regeneration: 3 HP/s after eight seconds without damage, capped at 50 HP;
bandages and wave supplies restore health above that. Maximum health is 100.

EQUIPMENT AND HAZARDS
M67: 2s fuse, 180 radial damage over 7m, 1s use cooldown; cover blocks blast.
Bandage: 35 HP after 2.4s, maximum 100 HP, two uses. Slot changes cancel healing
without refund. Healing use slows movement and blocks firing.
Flash: 1.1s player fuse, 7m radius, 5s knockdown; victims remain damageable.
Survivors visually rise after the status expires. Nearby players get a short veil
(2s from enemy flash / 1s from their own); NPC knockdown stays five seconds.
Boogie: 1.1s fuse, 7m radius, 5s original procedural Gangnam-style horse-riding
dance (crossed reins, alternating hops and lasso gesture). No song recording used.
Flash/boogie need unobstructed effect reach. Stun and dance use independent expiry
timestamps; reapplication extends the corresponding effect without locking AI.
Smoke: 5.5m radius sphere / 10s lifetime / fades fully during final second.
First entry into each cloud gives NPCs five seconds of blindness. Cloud obstruction
continues to block NPC aim after blindness ends. Player vision has a dense veil
inside the cloud; translucent sphere shells obscure it from outside.
Enemy and player smoke use identical rules and affect ALL NPC factions and the
player regardless of owner. No infrared immunity. Multiple cloud timers coexist.
Natural maximum is eleven live clouds; defensive capacity is twelve, without
evicting existing clouds. Blindness may outlast a cloud entered late in its life.
Mine: 1.5s arming delay, 15 HP, shootable; 4.5m blast radius; up to 45 player damage;
8s planting cooldown + 1s placement animation, global cap 6; no placement within
3m of the player (planning requires 4m). Visible body and blinking armed light.
Mines can chain react, do not count as living enemies, and clear at wave start.
Drone: 65 HP, large hitbox, audible flight, accelerating warning at 7s, strike at
10s for exactly 50% of max health. A strike resolves once and removes the drone.
Temporary disabling effects delay its strike until recovery; it cannot attack blind.
Taser: 1s charge, 7m activation range, 7 damage + 1.5s slow. Marksman: 1.3s laser
telegraph, 22 damage, fixed target point that can be dodged. Cover interrupts both.
Other enemy special throws have a 1s warning and 12s cooldown.

ROCKET MOBILITY UPDATE 09
Player-fired RPGs aimed downward (direction Y below -0.55) can launch from a world
surface beneath the player, within 2.2m horizontally and 2.5m below the feet. The blast
requires clear sight to the player. Enemy rockets, aerial detonations and side blasts
do not activate it. Rockets sweep their path and check the muzzle to prevent tunneling.
Launch: 11.5 m/s vertical speed with 15 m/s squared gravity, about 4.2m actual lift
and 1.5 seconds airborne on level ground. Air movement is multiplied by 1.65 until
landing: walk 4.5 -> 7.425 m/s; sprint 7.4 -> 12.21 m/s. HUD and smooth FOV cue apply.
Qualifying blast self-damage: 10, unaffected by difficulty; practice remains harmless.
This reduced cost also applies when headroom blocks a qualifying launch. At lethal
health, death takes priority and no launch occurs. Normal RPG blast damage is unchanged.
Launch cancels sliding/crouching when standing clearance exists; low ceilings block it.
Ceilings stop upward travel and solid roofs/cover catch descending players. Landing,
death, menu exit, practice reset, invalid-position recovery and admin flight clear the
boost. Pause freezes it. Additional blasts cannot stack launch impulses while boosted.
RPG remains 1 loaded + 8 reserve, 4.2s reload. Save data and other weapon balance unchanged.
129 automated simulation checks pass, eight new rocket checks covering all four maps
at 30/60/144 FPS, ammunition use, boost movement/reset, each difficulty, single-impact
damage/launch, exclusion cases, ceilings, roof landings/relaunches, death/menu/pause,
invalid-position recovery, arena limits and thin-wall collision. Mocked WebGL only;
manual Opera gameplay, visual movement feel and real GPU performance remain unverified.
