  Accurate 65m/s bolts, slight gravity, 45m range and 1 + 18 ammunition unchanged.

  resupplying into it ends an active slide safely, including under low cover.
  Normal crouching and sliding with other selected slots remain available.

  accelerating mechanism sound follow the same duration; no firing before completion.

  equipment choices, practice selections, crate rewards and shop information.

  Removed loadout selections fall back to AR-4/Glock; backups retain retired ownership.

  Sustained base damage falls from 215.38 to 146.67 per second (31.9% reduction).
  24 + 72 ammo, 1.8s reload, 10m effective range, spread and recoil unchanged.
- All 13 non-sniper ranged weapons share the AR-4 reflex attachment (with mounting
  foot), centered red-dot overlay and 49-degree FOV. Melee/equipment have no scope.
  Sniper retains 28-degree FOV, existing scope, head damage and penetration.
- Removed 8% crate probability redistributed: Pump 3.50 -> 5.50%, Sawed-Off 3 -> 4.50%,
  Chainsaw 3 -> 4.50%, Molotov 3.50 -> 5%, Throwing Knives 4 -> 5.50%.
  All other probabilities and rarities unchanged, including RPG at 0.10%.
- Regression checks cover every gun's aim ray (hitscan, flame and projectile), zoom,
  enter/exit/switch, foreground suppression, shared optic meshes, Uzi damage/cadence/
  reload, every crate interval, removed-item rejection and old-save fresh-session reload.
  Additional checks cover LMG 1.5s timing/audio/restart, slide rejection/cancellation,
  crossbow body/head damage and reload, 300-fuel conservation, flame transparency,
  draw ordering, fading and render-state cleanup.
