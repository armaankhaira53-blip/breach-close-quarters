BREACH / WAVE EVENTS UPDATE 13

EVENT SYSTEM
One guarded chance roll at the first playing update of each survival wave: a value
below 0.05 triggers one uniformly selected event (four equal intervals). A manually
occupied event slot suppresses the roll. A completed or failed event retains the
wave's occupied slot; active events cannot overlap. Practice has no events.

Active objectives show their reward, status, progress, a screen-space tracking marker
and north-up locator map. They have no skip control or optional label. Objects and
marked actors also have luminous world markers. All timings use paused simulation
time. Run exit, death, restart and admin wave changes cancel pending effects.

THIEF: Converts a living current-faction soldier without adding to the wave budget.
Runs at 3.6m/s (increased from 1.1m/s after user testing) along a validated route of
at least 24 navigation steps to an exit. Unarmed, carrying a large planked wooden
crate in both hands, with side handles, cross braces and visible fasteners. Its
pose follows his heading and status animations; the dropped supplies use the same
crate model. The dead thief does not regain a weapon.
Death drops supplies; walking within 1.6m with a clear line of sight collects them
for exactly 900 credits. Escape removes the soldier from the live count without a
kill or event reward. After the last enemy dies, a 12-second collection countdown
holds the next wave. Timeout removes supplies and fails the objective.

CRATE: Reachable location; 240 health with HUD and world health bars. Half of living
faction soldiers (rounded up), capped at five, attack it. Their attacks deal 12 damage
every 1.6s within 3m and clear sight. Other enemies keep their existing AI. Successful
defense awards 1,500 credits on wave clear, including the final-kill path before
same-frame player death. A destroyed crate fails without ending the run.

BEACON: 30-second countdown, hold E for 15 continuous seconds within 2.3m and clear
sight. Releasing E or leaving range resets progress. Disable pays 900 credits.
On expiry, a visible missile descends for 1.8s, then creates a two-second fireball,
an initial 0.65-second fading lighting flash and the existing synthesized boom.
After the latest user correction, impact removes exactly half of current health
once anywhere on the map, independent of difficulty, cover, height and sandbox
invincibility. It does not turn off invincibility for other damage. The objective
warns there is no safe zone and the locator marks the entire map as threatened.
No event credits on failure. An active
countdown or falling missile holds the next wave; ending the run cancels it.

RADIO: Converts a faction soldier and reserves five other unhurt, unoccupied soldiers
over 8m from the player, removing them from active play until the call completes.
The call lasts 10 seconds. Early operator death pays 500 credits and releases the
reservations permanently. Failure restores those five existing roster members as
reinforcements. Operator death after failure pays no event bonus. These enemies
remain ordinary wave enemies after the event fails, to be eliminated for wave clear.
Original faction, variant and total enemy limits remain unchanged.
While calling, the operator holds a detailed handheld radio near his face, with an
antenna, speaker grille and lit display. The prop and raised hand share the actor
pose through heading changes, dancing and knockdown. His weapon is hidden during
the call and returns if the call finishes; a killed operator leaves an unarmed body.

ADMIN CONTROLS
Existing Sandbox Powers panel: select one of the four named events, then click
START SELECTED EVENT. It bypasses chance but checks wave, occupancy, event type and
feasibility before modifying gameplay. Paused active runs are accepted because the
admin panel itself pauses the game. Rejected requests explain the reason.

REFILL ALL AMMO restores every item in the current three-slot loadout, including
unselected weapons, to magazine and wave-scaled reserve capacity. Fuel, bolts,
shells, rockets and consumables use their existing configured values; RPG remains
1 + 8. Planted C4 stays placed and counts toward the total three-charge supply.
Reload/burst/healing/melee work in progress is canceled; switch, fire and equipment
cooldowns, current loadout, health and projectiles are preserved. HUD values refresh
immediately. This is a one-time refill, not unlimited ammunition.
